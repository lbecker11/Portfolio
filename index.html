About

I'm a data analyst and math enthusiast focused on quantitative research, data science, financial systems, and computer science. My research spans statistical modelling for sports, systematic trading strategy development, and application of ML in different environments.

On top, I enjoy building my own applications combining C/C++ and Python. I believe understanding the systems you build at a low level makes you a better designer of them at a high level. This is why I think it's always a good idea to try to look into the black-box.

I keep a bookshelf of everything I've read. Zoom in and hover over a book to get a short take, click to read a full review. If you think there's something I should read that isn't on there, I'd love to hear it.
