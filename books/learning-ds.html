Learning Data Science

Sam Lau, Joseph Gonzalez, Deborah Nolan

Overview

Learning Data Science covers all the fundamental bases. You learn about data sources, working with pandas and SQL, designing experiments, and pulling summary statistics from them. You also learn about regression and classifier models, as well as a lot of basics about working with data.

Key Takeaways

The key takeaways for me was the good exaplanations on random variables. Going into detail how we get them, and how they are used in the real world.

Who Should Read This

Anyone who wants to get a good grip on what data science is about should read this to start building a good fundamental starting point.

Rating

4 out of 5, it does not go into extrem levels of details but thats not what you need in an introductory book.
