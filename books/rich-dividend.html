Get Rich with Dividends

Marc Lichtenfield

Overview

In this book Lichtenfield shares his system for dividend reinvesting. Just like Graham in a way, Lichtenfield looks for companies that have good fundamentals and have continuously raised their dividend. The idea is to invest in these companies and keep reinvesting the paid dividend to not only benefit from the usual compounding but also introduce dividend compounding to the mix. He outlines different risk plays, what to pay attention to when investing in companies with high dividends, what tax benefits you could claim in the US, and option plays to add additional income. Overall, this adds well to anyone who wants to build long term positions and have their investments pay an actual income without needing to sell their positions.

Key Takeaways

  • >4.7% dividend yield
  • <75% payout ratio
  • Continuous dividend growth over the past 10 years
  • Pay attention to an increased payout ratio, decline in cash flow, earnings, or sales, and any change in dividend policy

Who Should Read This

If you care about long term investing, dividend companies are for you and this book outlines an easy system that could be followed.

Rating

4 out of 5. Easy and short read with lots of easy to process information. I liked the idea of introducing option plays like cash covered calls to long term positions in case a company no longer fits your criteria. Definitely something I will want to adopt.
