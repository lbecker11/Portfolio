Linear Algebra

Mike X. Cohen

Overview

Linear Algebra by Cohen is a really good book to review or start your journey in linear algebra. It perfectly builds it up from the ground, starting at scalars, going to vectors, and then into matrices. From there Cohen starts to expand on linear equations, regression, prediction, factor analysis, matrix spaces, and much more. Each chapter includes practice problems and how to code the solutions. This was my first maths book read outside of university to refresh and upgrade my linear algebra knowledge and it was the best choice. If you read this, work through the examples, and complete the code questions you will be in good shape to understand any linear algebra concept that comes your way in the future. This is essential because a lot of ML algorithms rely on it. If you start with this and then work your way through the data science books, everything will click much more easily since you already have a visual in your head.

Key Takeaways

SVD and PCA.

Who Should Read This

If you want to start linear algebra for your own enjoyment or to understand why ML does what it does.

Rating

5 out of 5. I thoroughly enjoyed it, and I had a question about a topic, emailed the author, and he actually replied.
