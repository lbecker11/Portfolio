Data Science from Scratch

Joel Grus

Overview

Data science from scratch is probably one of my favourites that I have read. Joel gives a fantastic introduction into Python and then explains all the mathematical concepts and algorithms in just enough detail to make ther reader understand why we would use it. Because Joel's writting is so fun, I also had a look at his "I don't like notebooks" talk which I thought was very entertaining and connects well with the "Software Engineering for Data Scientists" book.

Key Takeaways

Its hard to pick just a few key takeaways as the entire book covers so many topics so well. If Im being honest, any reader would do themselves a favour to just read the whole book.

Who Should Read This

Anyone who wants to know how machine learning algorithms work under the hood.

Rating

5 out of 5, not much more to add.
