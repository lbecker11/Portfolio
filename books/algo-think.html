Algorithmic Thinking

Daniel Zingaro

Overview

Algorithmic Thinking by Zingaro is a fantastic book explaining data structures and algorithms in C. Because the entire book is in C, you will understand every single detail about how to solve problems at a low level. You will understand how hashing works, how stacks work, how queues work, how priority queues are min/max heaps, all kinds of graphs, and much more. Reading this before the Competitive Programmer's Handbook is ideal since it goes into much more detail and is in C rather than C++. Each chapter includes sample problems that are worked through, and afterwards you can look up the problem and try it yourself. This book again confirms to me that No Starch Press produces fantastic books. Each author is able to explain concepts in simple terms that anyone can follow, and Daniel Zingaro is no exception.

Key Takeaways

Stacks, min/max heaps, segment trees.

Who Should Read This

If you want a better understanding of data structures and algorithms and how they really work from the ground up, this is it.

Rating

5 out of 5.
