Million Dollar Math

Hugh Barker

Overview

Million Dollar Math is how I wished math was introduced to me in high school. I read this book 7 years ago before I was interested in mathematics. I looked through the book store at the airport in Zurich. The book covers topics about exponential growth, casino games, gambling systems, investing, hacking and more. For me the chapters about gambling strategies and investing have been the most interesting and are definitely a favourite to re-read for a high level overview. It is really interesting how a lot of topics and "characters" keep recurring. For example John Larry Kelly, who invented the Kelly criterion for smart bet sizing, is mentioned here, in The Man Who Solved the Market, and is also used a lot in my own analysis. The investing part covers a lot of metrics but also trading strategies like pairs trading and arbitrage. Definitely a good read.

Key Takeaways

Financial trading systems, betting strategies, probability.

Who Should Read This

Anyone who wants to know more about how to use money to become wealthy (or poor).

Rating

5 out of 5. I will re-read it again.
