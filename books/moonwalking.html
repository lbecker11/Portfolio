Moonwalking with Einstein

Joshua Foer

Overview

Moonwalking with Einstein follows the story of Joshua Foer into his research of the world memory championships. You learn about how strong our memory used to be, how paper has replaced having a good memory, and how ancient memory techniques are still used today by memory champions and students who want to get the most out of their exams. The key here are mnemonic techniques like memory palaces and person-action-object sequences to create a path in your mind that you can always revisit to remember whatever you want. The book also goes into stories of people who had extraordinary memories and people who, due to sickness or injury, had their memory altered to be stuck in a certain period. The entire book is an inspiring story about human memory, how we evolved to use it less, and how we can train it to remember more again.

Key Takeaways

Memory palaces and other mnemonic techniques can help achieve great things.

Who Should Read This

If you have read Make It Stick and want to learn more about how you can push your memory, this is a book that is not about the how but the why.

Rating

4 out of 5, the chapter on attacking Daniel Tammet was a bit strange.
