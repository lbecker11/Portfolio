Rich Dad Poor Dad

Robert Kiyosaki

Overview

I saw this book at the airport in Gatwick and thought might as well, since it is on many "to read" lists. I must say whether you read the book or not, the outcome is the same. Considering Kiyosaki is now heavily in debt, he should have probably taken his own advice. This book covers his story where he compares his dad to his friend's dad, who takes risks and is a good investor. He goes into detail about how grades do not get you paid and only real hard work gets you to where you want to be. He outlines schemes he used to purchase and sell houses. I do not think most of these things work in today's world and when it comes to real investment strategies, there is not much in here.

Key Takeaways

The message is simply to work hard.

Who Should Read This

I don't know if anyone really needs to read this.

Rating

2 out of 5.
