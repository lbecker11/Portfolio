The Man Who Solved the Market

Gregory Zuckerman

Overview

In this book Gregory Zuckerman narrates the life story of Jim Simmons and the founding members of Renaissance Technologies. While the exact model that they use remains a mystery, we do get a peak into the thinking and features might flow into it.

Key Takeaways

A lot of the algorithms that we use for several problems stem from the members that have worked in Simmons fund. For me it was interesting to see that the Kelly criterion was referenced as this is something I have worked with myself. On top, I also fully agree with the saying in the book that it is impossible to predict a price of product but we can predict how it might react to certain stimuli.

Who Should Read This

I thought this book was very motivating to dig deeper into quantitative research and learning about all aspects that could make a successful trading model.

Rating

4 out of 5, as Zuckerman did use some weird narrations in some parts.
