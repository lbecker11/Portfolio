Bayesian Statistics the Fun Way

Will Kurt

Overview

This book covered the basics of Bayesian statistics. When it comes to ML, Bayesian statistics is the key concept to know. The book does a really good job at introducing basic probability, different distributions like beta and binomial, and then transitions into Bayes' theorem. What's good is that you immediately get introduced to the correct Python syntax and libraries to perform all needed calculations. One of the best examples in the book is probably explaining how Bayes' theorem works using Lego pieces.

Key Takeaways

PMF, PDF, and CDF. Everything is explained so well and with brilliant examples. The appendix also covers the basics of linear algebra and calculus that you would need to understand how an area under the curve is calculated.

Who Should Read This

If you need a simple introduction to Bayes' theorem and why it's important, this book is definitely a good starting point.

Rating

4 out of 5. I would have given it 5 if at the end there was a quick section of additional readings. These usually help me to know where to take my reading next.
