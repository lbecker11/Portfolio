Make It Stick

Peter C. Brown, Henry L. Roediger III, Mark A. McDaniel

Overview

Recently I went on a quick journey into how to improve learning and memory. I went looking through a lot of research papers and advice. Make It Stick takes all the concepts and puts them in one book. Throughout, you learn a lot about how spaced repetition is key, how learning needs to be hard and deliberate, how interleaving topics instead of massed practice is the better option, and how structural learning is best done by trying to identify the high level architecture rather than getting lost in the details. It also goes into detail about mnemonics and recommends Moonwalking with Einstein as a good follow-up. Since then I have tried to apply a lot of these concepts to my own learning routines. I spend more time trying to understand the high level concepts than getting lost in the details, visualising concepts since we can remember images better than words on a page, using spaced repetition, and avoiding linear notes. I have also been following Justin Sung's videos on mind maps, but I think these work better for conceptual books than textbooks. I will most definitely continue to research cognitive science to learn better and faster.

Key Takeaways

Interleave topics, perform spaced repetition, and visualise each concept.

Who Should Read This

If you want a good overview of how efficient studying is done, this is the one.

Rating

5 out of 5, I would definitely recommend this book.
