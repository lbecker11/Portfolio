Essential Mathematics for Data Science

Thomas Nield

Overview

This book covers the basic math concepts needed to understand some of the most used machine learning algorithms. It covers number theory and calculus, probability, descriptive and inferential statistics, linear algebra, linear regression, logistic classification, and neural networks.

Key Takeaways

I think the chapter on neural networks was really good and helped me understand the math behind them. This book chapter was also one of the reasons why I further wanted to dive into calculus.

Who Should Read This

Anyone who wants to brush up on their math essentials.

Rating

4 out of 5.
