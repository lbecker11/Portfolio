Damodaran on Valuation

Aswath Damodaran

Overview

During my studies I had the chance to represent my university in the local CFA round. One book that was recommended to me was Damodaran on Valuation. Not only was I able to use it during the competition but also for my thesis. Damodaran goes into great detail about discounted cash flow models to help estimate a company's real value, relative valuation looking at multiples, and loose ends in valuations. Loose ends refers to values of things that are hard to measure: different holdings, employee options, intangibles, synergy, management, and so on. Damodaran includes many useful examples and explains how the formulas change depending on what you pay attention to. As I work on my own long term investments this is definitely a book I want to reread once my overall understanding has improved further. Pairing it with other classics like The Intelligent Investor and Security Analysis, you can definitely build good principles and concepts for your own investment strategies for long term growth.

Key Takeaways

You can value companies in a lot of different ways and there is a lot to pay attention to. Damodaran gives you some good pointers.

Who Should Read This

If you are interested in financial valuations and want to learn how to value equities, this is the starting point.

Rating

5 out of 5 for a good introductory book.
