Dividend Growth Machine

Nathan Winklepleck

Overview

Dividend Growth Machine is a logical complement to Get Rich With Dividends. It is a short book outlining why you should invest in companies that have been paying dividends consistently over time. I must say you are probably better off reading Get Rich With Dividends first because it goes into much better depth on the concepts. This book just touches on most things. It covers why dividends are good, why you can ignore Mr. Market since that is not the reason you are investing, and how compounding is affected.

Key Takeaways

Good examples with tips on how to get started.

Who Should Read This

If you care about dividend investing, this could aid that journey.

Rating

3 out of 5, not much unheard of information in here.
